About VacciCare+

Our Mission

VacciCare+ is a simple, frontend-only solution to help families and healthcare providers manage child vaccination schedules and appointments. It uses localStorage to save data for demo and offline use.

Modules
  • Parent registration, child records, appointments, QR generation, reminders
  • Hospital registration, approvals, appointments, QR scanning
  • Admin oversight, approvals, CSV export, feedback review

Note: This is a demo without backend. Data stays in your browser.
